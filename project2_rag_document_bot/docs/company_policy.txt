# Global SaaS Tech Solutions - Remote Work & Equipment Policy

## 1. Remote Work Eligibility
All full-time software engineers, product managers, and support staff are eligible for hybrid or full-time remote work after completing their 30-day initial onboarding period.

## 2. Hardware Stipend & Laptop Policy
Each remote employee is entitled to a one-time remote home office setup stipend of $1,200 USD. This covers ergonomic desks, external monitors, and peripherals.
Standard issue work laptops (Apple MacBook Pro M3 or Dell XPS 15) remain the sole property of Global SaaS Tech Solutions and must be returned upon employment termination.

## 3. Work Hours & Timezone Expectation
Employees must maintain core working availability between 10:00 AM and 3:00 PM in their local primary timezone. All client emergencies must be reported in the #incident-response Slack channel.
